# YT Player

A simple YouTube video player for [Android](#android), [Android TV](#android-tv) and [ChromeOS](#chromeos) with features such as no ads, background playback and more!

</div><br>

Features:
- No Ads
- Background Playback
- Picture In Picture
- Subtitles (English only)
- [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app/) (Only auto skips sponsors)

<br>

## Android

Requires: Android version 8 and above

Usage:
- Press the "Share" button on any video in the YouTube app, press the "More" button and press "YT Player".

<br>

## Android TV

Requires: Android version 7.1 and above

Usage:
- Launch the "YT Player" app, open a browser on any device and visit the url shown on screen with the YouTube video url passed in the ?url= parameter.

Notice:
- Picture In Picture is only supported on devices with the modified capability such as Nvidia Shield TV.
- Picture In Picture also requires Android version 8 and above for it to work on devices such as Nvidia Shield TV.
- Devices that use an os like Google TV don't support Picture In Picture as its unsupported on those os.

<br>

## ChromeOS

Requires: Android version 8 and above

Usage:
- Copy the YouTube video url to your clipboard then launch the "YT Player" app.
